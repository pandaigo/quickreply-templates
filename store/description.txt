Stop retyping the same messages. QuickReply Templates lets you save, organize, and insert text templates in seconds — right from the right-click menu.

Perfect for customer support agents, sales reps, e-commerce sellers, and anyone who sends repetitive messages every day.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Click the extension icon and create a template.
2. Right-click any text field on any website.
3. Select your template — it's inserted instantly.

That's it. No account needed, no cloud sync, no data leaves your device.

KEY FEATURES

Right-Click Insert
Right-click any text box, email composer, or chat input and pick a template from the menu. One click, done.

Text Shortcuts (Pro)
Assign a shortcut like /thanks or ;ty to any template. Type the shortcut in any text field and it auto-expands into the full text. No right-click needed.

Smart Variables
Use {date}, {time}, or {today} to auto-fill the current date and time. Create your own variables like {name}, {company}, {product}, or anything you want — the extension asks you to fill them in each time.

Import & Export (Pro)
Back up your templates as a JSON file. Share templates with your team. Move them between computers.

Remember Values
Turn on "Remember values" and the extension remembers what you typed for each variable, per template. Next time, it's pre-filled.
